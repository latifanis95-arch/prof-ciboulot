Privacy Policy

Data accessed

When the Owner connects one of his TikTok accounts, the Tool receives, through TikTok's official OAuth flow: basic profile information (user.info.basic: display name, avatar, open ID) and permission to send videos to that account's TikTok inbox as drafts (video.upload), which the Owner then finishes and posts in the TikTok app.

How it is used

This data is used only to show which of his accounts is connected and to publish the Owner's own videos when he clicks Publish. It is never sold, shared or used for advertising or analytics.

Storage

Access tokens are stored locally on the Owner's computer only, in a file readable only by his user account. No server or database operated by a third party stores this data.

Other users

The Tool does not collect any data about other TikTok users or viewers.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions. Local tokens are then deleted.
