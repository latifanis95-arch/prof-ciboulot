Ciboulot Studio

Ciboulot Studio is a private desktop tool used by one creator, the author of the TikTok account Professeur Ciboulot, to publish his own short educational videos about science, the brain and learning.

How it works

  1. The creator records the voice-over and the video is edited on his own computer.
  2. He reviews the finished video and explicitly clicks Publish.
  3. The tool uses TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API to send the video to his own TikTok inbox as a draft; he finishes and posts it in the TikTok app. Nothing is ever posted without his explicit action.

Ciboulot Studio is not a public service: it does not offer accounts to other users and does not access anyone else's data.
